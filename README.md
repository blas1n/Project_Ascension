# README.md

# Project Codename: Project_Ascension

> A Civilization-Driven MMOFPS where players expand humanity's frontier against a world that constantly pushes back.

---

## Vision

This project is not a theme park MMORPG.

This project is not a survival sandbox.

This project is not a city builder.

This project is a living world simulation where:

* Players expand civilization
* NPCs preserve civilization
* The World Will erodes civilization

The game revolves around:

* Exploration
* Discovery
* Contracts
* Organizations
* Settlements
* Civilization Growth
* MMOFPS Combat

---

## Core Philosophy

### Civilization is the Main Character

Players are important.

Cities are important.

Organizations are important.

But ultimately.

Civilization itself is the protagonist.

---

### No Traditional Level System

Players do not gain power primarily through levels.

Players grow through:

* Discoveries
* Knowledge
* Reputation
* Influence
* Organizations
* Settlements

---

### Contracts Replace Quests

Every responsibility in the world is represented as a contract.

Examples:

* Monster Hunt
* Survey Mission
* Citizenship
* Governorship
* Ownership
* Emperor

Even political power is a contract.

---

### Discovery Creates Progress

Knowledge is discovered.

Knowledge is traded.

Knowledge evolves.

Every discovery can become the foundation of future discoveries.

---

### Players Create Conditions

Players do not place buildings manually.

Players create conditions.

Civilization grows naturally.

---

## World Structure

Four surviving civilization zones surround the ruins of the fallen Central Empire.

### Northwest

Military Civilization

* Snow
* Fortresses
* Firearms

---

### Northeast

Arcane Civilization

* Crystal Desert
* Spellcraft
* Ancient Knowledge

---

### Southwest

Nature Civilization

* Jungle
* Biotech
* Spores

---

### Southeast

Traditional Civilization

* Grasslands
* Waterfalls
* Melee Combat
* Archery

---

Between civilizations lie frontier regions.

Toward the center lies increasing danger.
